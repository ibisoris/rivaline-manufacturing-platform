# Business case

Scenario: a fictional UK coatings manufacturer lacks a shared view of customer demand,
stock, batch progress and quality. Manual reconciliation delays decisions.

Objective: demonstrate trustworthy integration and operational visibility with a small,
explainable prototype. Stakeholders are synthetic sales, purchasing, warehouse, production,
quality and operations roles. Phase 1 delivers foundations only.

Next evidence: measure reconciliation effort, data completeness and decision turnaround on
synthetic scenarios. Do not present hypothetical savings as realised benefits.
