# Benefits and KPIs (planned)

order fulfilment lead time, on-time dispatch and batch quality pass rate.

Define numerator, denominator, grain, time window and exclusions before implementation.
Compare synthetic before/after scenarios with reproducible inputs. Phase 1 measures no
operational benefit and provides no dashboard; do not invent baseline or realised savings.
